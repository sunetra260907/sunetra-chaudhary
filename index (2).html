01 / A little about me

About Sunetra

AI Trainer by profession, creative soul by nature — I enjoy learning, exploring, and expressing myself in different ways.

✦

Learning, technology & empowerment

As an AI Trainer, I guide college graduates as they explore low-code and no-code platforms. These tools make application development more approachable by reducing the amount of traditional programming required. I enjoy breaking complex ideas into understandable steps, encouraging hands-on learning, and showing how digital tools can solve everyday challenges. I believe technology should feel accessible to everyone, because great ideas can come from anyone. Outside work, dance, swimming, hiking, and reading give me different ways to recharge, explore, and express myself.
